2. The Regulatory Landscape

2.1 SEBI's ESG Mandate Evolution

2012 — BRR Introduced

Business Responsibility Report (BRR) mandated for top 100 listed companies. Qualitative, principles-based.

2021 — BRSR Replaces BRR

SEBI replaces BRR with BRSR. Quantitative disclosures, 9 NGRBC principles, structured format. Voluntary for FY21-22.

2022-23 — Mandatory for Top 1,000

BRSR becomes mandatory for top 1,000 listed companies by market cap. ₹100+ crore penalty risk for non-compliance.

2023-24 — BRSR Core & Assurance

BRSR Core introduced with 9 KPIs requiring reasonable assurance from independent auditors. Top 150 companies must get third-party assurance.

2026-27 — Extended to Top 250 + Value Chain

Assurance mandate expands to top 250. Value chain ESG disclosures become mandatory. Supply chain BRSR reporting triggers SME demand.

3. The Gap: Why Current Solutions Fail

3.1 The Manual Process (Status Quo)

Today, a typical BRSR compliance cycle at a mid-cap Indian company looks like this:

  1. Data Collection (8-12 weeks): Sustainability teams email 15-30 departments requesting data across environment, social, governance categories. No standardized internal templates.
  2. Report Drafting (4-6 weeks): Consultants or internal teams manually populate SEBI's Annexure I/II format in Word/Excel. Cross-referencing against previous year data.
  3. Gap Identification (2-4 weeks): Auditors review for completeness against 216 data points. Manual checking against BRSR Core's 9 assured KPIs.
  4. Cross-Framework Mapping (2-3 weeks): For dual-listed or multinational companies, mapping BRSR disclosures to ESRS, GRI, TCFD for international investor consumption.
  5. Board Approval & Filing (2-3 weeks): Final review, board sign-off, submission to BSE/NSE.

Total cycle time: 18-28 weeks (4.5-7 months)
Total cost: ₹15-50 lakhs (Big 4) or 400-800 internal person-hours

3.3 The Specific Pain Points FileBRSR Addresses

Pain Point 1: "Did we disclose everything?"

Companies cannot easily verify if their BRSR report covers all 216 mandatory data points. Missing even one BRSR Core field can trigger auditor qualifications and SEBI notices.

Pain Point 2: "How do we compare to peers?"

Sustainability heads lack real-time benchmarks against NIFTY 50 sector peers. Without benchmarks, boards approve mediocre disclosures that damage ESG ratings.

Pain Point 3: "What does our auditor need?"

BRSR Core assurance requires 9 specific KPIs with quantitative evidence. Companies don't know which of their existing disclosures satisfy these requirements until the auditor flags gaps.

Pain Point 4: "How does BRSR map to ESRS/GRI?"

Multinational Indian companies (Infosys, TCS, Reliance) must also file under EU CSRD and GRI. No tool provides automated cross-framework mapping with BRSR as the primary input.

Pain Point 5: "We have last year's report — extract our data"

Companies want to use their previously filed BRSR PDF as a baseline for gap analysis and year-over-year comparison. No existing tool can parse an unstructured BRSR PDF into structured data points.
